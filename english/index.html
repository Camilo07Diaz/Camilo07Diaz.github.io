About Me

I’m Camilo Díaz, a Data Analyst and Civil Engineer passionate about uncovering insights that drive smarter decisions. My professional path began in the construction industry, where I spent several years coordinating complex projects and managing multidisciplinary teams.

During my journey living and working across different countries, I discovered a growing interest in technology and data analytics. Through my Data Analytics program at TripleTen, I gained hands-on experience with Python, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, Looker Studio, and Advanced Excel.

Today, I combine my analytical skills with my engineering mindset to deliver insights that are actionable, evidence-based, and aligned with business goals. My goal is to keep growing in the data field, contributing to projects that create measurable impact.
